Cleveland Division of Police — Strategic Recommendation
CDP RTCC — Platform Assessment
Motorola CommandCentral Aware: Recommended Primary Platform
DECISIVE ADVANTAGE
Radio Infrastructure Lock
Cleveland's APX P25 radio fleet is a 15–25 year capital investment. Only Motorola provides native GPS telemetry and console integration — no other ecosystem can replicate this without significant parallel infrastructure cost.
DECISIVE ADVANTAGE
CAD Native Integration
PremierOne CAD feeds into CommandCentral Aware with zero latency and no middleware. Axon, Genetec, and Flock all require API bridges that introduce failure points and lag. For a city-scale RTCC, this difference is operationally significant.
DECISIVE ADVANTAGE
Avigilon AI Analytics
Avigilon's Appearance Search and Unusual Motion Detection represent the most mature AI video analytics in the market. Axon Vision (launched April 2026) is promising but unproven at scale. Genetec's analytics are solid. Flock is limited.
WATCH CLOSELY
Axon Threat: AI Velocity
Axon's Draft One, Axon Vision, and Axon 911 represent the fastest AI innovation velocity in the market. Motorola's gap on AI report writing is real. If Axon successfully integrates its 911 platform at scale, the competitive picture will shift significantly by 2027–2028.
RETAIN AS ASSET
Flock Safety LPR Network
Cleveland's 2,700+ Flock cameras are a real operational asset. The recommendation is not to rip them out — Flock LPR data feeds into CommandCentral Aware via API. Flock's vehicle search capability remains best-in-class and should be retained as a data source.
PHASE OUT
Fusus Subscription
With CommandCentral Aware handling camera aggregation, community cameras via CommandCentral Community, and event-driven automation natively, Fusus becomes redundant middleware. The subscription cost should be redirected to BRINC DFR dock deployment across Cleveland's five districts.
