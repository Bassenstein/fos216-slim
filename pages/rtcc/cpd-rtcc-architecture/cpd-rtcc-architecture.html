Section 01 · Integration Assessment

Yes — They Can Integrate, and the Case Is Compelling

Reveal Technology and Motorola CommandCentral Aware are not competing for the same architectural layer — they operate at opposite ends of the intelligence chain. Motorola owns the city-wide command infrastructure (CAD, 911, camera aggregation, unit tracking, cross-agency comms). Reveal owns the tactical edge (3D terrain, biometrics, offline AI, ATAK). A CPD-dedicated on-premise data center is the substrate that binds both — hosting Motorola's on-prem components, storing Reveal's geo-tagged encounter data, maintaining CJIS-compliant video archives, and providing the compute backbone for AI analytics across both platforms. The three elements are mutually reinforcing: each one is stronger because the other two exist.

Section 05 · Technical Integration Points

How Reveal Talks to Motorola via the Data Center

Five specific technical bridges that allow the platforms to function as a unified system rather than parallel silos.

🗺️
ATAK → Esri WMS Bridge
Farsight → CommandCentral
CPD Data Center runs an ATAK server that receives Farsight terrain tile exports. A middleware converter transforms ATAK tile packages into Esri WMS layers that CommandCentral Aware can natively consume as a custom GIS overlay — placing SWAT's 3D tactical picture directly on the RTCC analyst's screen.
🧬
Identifi → RMS Sync
Biometrics → Records
When Identifi field contacts reconnect to CPD LTE/WiFi, geo-tagged biometric encounter records push via REST API to CPD Data Center. Records are cross-referenced against PremierOne RMS and appended to subject files — giving detectives a complete contact history with GPS timestamps.
📡
CommandCentral → DC Video Archive
Video → Storage
Motorola's Safety Hub edge appliances ingest community partner cameras (businesses, schools, city CCTV). Rather than relying solely on Motorola's Azure cloud, CPD Data Center acts as a local video archive — maintaining CJIS-compliant on-prem storage of all investigative video evidence with zero-copy evidentiary integrity.
🚨
ShotSpotter → AI Enrichment → COP
Sensor → Analytics → Display
ShotSpotter alerts enter CommandCentral in real time. CPD Data Center AI layer simultaneously runs a correlation job: prior incidents at that location, active warrants in the zone, Identifi contact history, LPR reads in the last hour. Enriched intelligence card surfaces in the analyst's Missions workspace within 30 seconds of the alert.
🔗
ATAK Mutual Federation
CPD + FBI + USMS + ATF
CPD Data Center hosts a TAK Server that federates with federal agency TAK networks. Reveal Farsight data and Identifi biometric alerts visible on CPD RTCC are simultaneously pushed to FBI HRT and USMS tactical operations centers — creating a true joint operations common picture without requiring a shared Motorola subscription.
🧠
Unified Data Lake for AI
Cross-Platform Analytics
CPD Data Center's data lake ingests from both platforms: Reveal encounter records + 3D terrain data + Motorola CAD/RMS/video + LPR + ShotSpotter. ML models trained on this unified dataset produce predictive patrol recommendations, crime pattern alerts, and resource allocation insights that neither platform could generate independently.

Section 07 · Regulatory Compliance

Compliance Framework — CJIS, FedRAMP, Consent Decree

FBI CJIS SP v6.0
Governs all CJI storage, access, encryption, audit logging, personnel screening. Every component of this stack — Reveal, Motorola, and the DC — must meet or exceed CJIS SP requirements. Motorola is already CJIS-compliant on Azure; DC handles on-prem CJIS obligations.
FedRAMP / StateRAMP
Motorola CommandCentral is deployable in FedRAMP and StateRAMP authorized cloud environments. CPD Data Center, as a CJIS-compliant on-prem facility, can host Motorola components locally, providing CPD full data sovereignty over criminal justice information.
DOJ Consent Decree
Motorola's Missions audit trail and BWC auto-activation directly satisfy use-of-force documentation requirements. Reveal's Identifi geo-tagged encounter logs satisfy stop accountability obligations. The DC's 7-year retention policy satisfies evidence preservation requirements for litigation.
Reveal DoD ATO
Reveal's Farsight and Identifi hold DoD network Authority to Operate — enabling CPD participation in federal task force operations on classified networks. The ATAK federation link between CPD DC and federal TAK servers is ATO-covered.
SOC 2 Type II / ISO 27001
CPD Data Center should target SOC 2 Type II audit within 18 months of operation — aligning with Motorola's own audit framework and satisfying oversight requirements from City Council, City Council's IT committee, and the DOJ monitoring team.
Ohio Public Records Law
BWC and RTCC footage subject to Ohio Public Records Act (ORC § 149.43). CPD DC must implement automated redaction workflows for FOIA responses — commercial AI redaction tools (e.g., Motorola Redaction Studio) integrate directly into the evidence workflow.
