Power Architecture Philosophy — Grid-Independent, City-Sovereign

This revision replaces the Cummins diesel generator specified in Rev 1.0 with a dual-technology, fuel-flexible primary generation system: Mainspring Energy Linear Generators as baseload/prime power, and Hyliion KARNO Power Modules as dispatchable peaker and N+1 redundancy. The Cleveland Public Power (CPP) grid connection is retained but demoted to cold standby — the data center operates grid-independent 24/7. This eliminates fuel storage risk, eliminates diesel exhaust permitting, enables zero-carbon operation as hydrogen becomes available on the Dominion Energy Ohio gas grid, and aligns with the City of Cleveland's 2030 carbon neutrality commitment. The ArtCraft building's existing natural gas service from Dominion Energy Ohio provides the primary fuel pathway, with methanol and hydrogen blend-in capability through the Mainspring and KARNO control systems' real-time fuel switching.

01 Fuel Hierarchy & Switching Logic
Four-Tier Fuel Priority Stack

Both the Mainspring LGen and KARNO systems operate from the same multi-fuel supply manifold. The building management system (BMS) monitors fuel availability and quality in real time, directing both generator platforms to the highest-priority available fuel simultaneously. Fuel transitions occur live, under load, with zero interruption to power output.

PRIORITY 1 Green Hydrogen (H₂) Blend via Dominion Energy Ohio H₂ pilot · 100% H₂ ready (both platforms) · zero carbon emissions
PRIORITY 2 Methanol On-site storage tank (ISO tank) · e-methanol or bio-methanol pathway · near-zero NOx · no SOx
PRIORITY 3 Natural Gas (NG) Dominion Energy Ohio utility gas · ArtCraft building existing 4" main · 5–15 psig · always available
PRIORITY 4 CPP Grid (Standby) Only triggered if all on-site generation offline · ATS auto-transfer <100ms · bridged by UPS
MAINSPRING FUEL SWITCHING NOTE

Mainspring LGen units transition between natural gas, biogas, and hydrogen blends in real time without downtime. The Northport, NY hydrogen deployment (expected operational September 2026) validates 100% H₂ operation commercially. The CPD system can begin on natural gas and transition to hydrogen blends as Dominion Energy Ohio expands its H₂ injection program on the Northeast Ohio distribution system, requiring zero hardware modification.

KARNO FUEL SWITCHING NOTE

The KARNO flameless oxidation architecture is compatible with fuels ranging from ammonia (slow flame) to hydrogen (fast flame) without any hardware modification. Demonstrated live switching between natural gas, nitrogen-rich syngas, and majority-hydrogen blends with no recalibration or shutdown. The KARNO's native 800V DC output architecture is aligned with NVIDIA's AI data center power specification — enabling direct DC coupling to GPU rack power supplies and eliminating one AC/DC conversion stage.

Methanol Storage & Supply System

Methanol serves as a renewable liquid energy carrier that dramatically reduces on-site storage complexity compared to hydrogen (no cryogenic or high-pressure storage required). A single 10,000-gallon ISO storage tank installed on the ArtCraft building roof or adjacent ground-level pad provides approximately 14 days of autonomous operation at full load. E-methanol (produced from green hydrogen + captured CO₂) or bio-methanol supply contracts can be established with local distributors operating through the Port of Cleveland's liquid bulk terminal infrastructure.

REDUNDANCY ARCHITECTURE

Normal operation: 2× Mainspring LGen (500 kW) + 1× KARNO (200 kW) = 700 kW active. IT load: 350–450 kW. If one Mainspring unit fails, the second KARNO auto-starts within 10 seconds: 250 kW (LGen-B) + 200 kW (KARNO-A) + 200 kW (KARNO-B) = 650 kW — still above the 450 kW design IT load with margin. If both Mainspring units fail, 2× KARNO = 400 kW, sufficient for the 350 kW base IT load. CPP grid engages only if the entire on-site fleet goes offline simultaneously — a 1-in-10,000-hour probability given each unit's MTBF.

Power Flow — Microgrid Architecture
CHP — Combined Heat & Power Integration

Both the Mainspring LGen and KARNO Power Modules are CHP-capable, capturing waste heat from the generation process. The Mainspring system achieves greater than 80% total thermal efficiency in CHP mode. At the ArtCraft building, recovered heat (approximately 200–300 kW thermal) can offset building HVAC load in winter months, reducing the data center's cooling parasitic draw and improving overall site PUE to an estimated 1.15 — well below the 1.35 target of the diesel architecture.

UPS — Role Change from Rev 1.0

In Rev 1.0, the UPS system bridged from CPP grid to diesel generator start (typically 8–15 seconds). In this architecture, the UPS bridges two different transitions: (1) any single on-site generator unit failure until the standby unit ramps (approximately 10 seconds for KARNO cold start), and (2) the very rare full on-site generation failure until CPP ATS transfer completes (under 100ms via static transfer switch). The UPS specification is therefore reduced from the 2× 250kVA units in Rev 1.0 to 2× 200kVA, as bridging time requirements are shorter.

The Mainspring LGen produces near-zero NOx (less than 0.02 lb/MWh) without aftertreatment, meeting South Coast AQMD and Ohio EPA air quality standards out of the box. This eliminates the selective catalytic reduction (SCR) systems required for diesel and natural gas ICE generators, simplifying permitting and reducing maintenance. The KARNO's flameless oxidation process similarly requires no aftertreatment. Ohio EPA air permit for this system will be a minor facility permit rather than the major source review required for diesel installations of equivalent capacity.

CITY OF CLEVELAND 2030 CARBON COMMITMENT

This power architecture directly supports Cleveland's Sustainable Cleveland 2030 Action Plan. On natural gas today: approximately 97% lower NOx than diesel, and approximately 35% lower CO₂ than grid power (Ohio grid still coal/gas heavy). On methanol blend: near-zero NOx, carbon-neutral if bio-methanol sourced. On green hydrogen: fully net-zero operation. The infrastructure requires zero hardware changes to transition between these fuels as availability and economics evolve. The federal Investment Tax Credit (ITC) of 30% applies to both Mainspring LGen and KARNO installations through 2032.

7-YEAR OPERATING COST ADVANTAGE

The Mainspring + KARNO system eliminates diesel fuel costs (~$185,000/year at 72-hr emergency tank annual test cycles and standby runs), eliminates annual diesel fuel maintenance ($28,000/year), and earns approximately $45,000/year in demand response revenue from the CPP grid through its export-capable microgrid interconnection. Over 7 years, this is approximately $1.8M in operating savings — more than offsetting the net CapEx premium of $706,700. 7-year TCO for Rev 2.0 power system: $2.28M CapEx + $3.2M OpEx (fuel + maintenance) = $5.48M vs Rev 1.0 at $1.57M CapEx + $4.9M OpEx = $6.47M. Net 7-year savings: approximately $990,000.
