01 Executive Summary

This document specifies the design of a dedicated, city-owned public safety data center to be installed within the new Cleveland Division of Police (CDP) headquarters at the ArtCraft Building, downtown Cleveland. The facility will serve as the sole sovereign infrastructure for the Street Takeover Detection System (STDS), CommandCentral Aware data feeds, Avigilon Unity video management, and Skydio DFR Command telemetry — maintaining complete City of Cleveland and CPD data custody under CJIS Security Policy v6.0.

SOVEREIGNTY REQUIREMENT

No STDS incident data, acoustic audio evidence, Avigilon video, LPR captures, drone telemetry, or Criminal Justice Information (CJI) shall be processed or stored on vendor-operated cloud infrastructure. All data remains within city-owned and city-operated hardware at the ArtCraft facility. Vendor platforms (CommandCentral Aware, Avigilon Unity, Skydio DFR Command) operate in on-premises or local-cloud modes connected to this facility.

KEY STRATEGIC BENEFIT

City ownership eliminates perpetual SaaS licensing exposure for storage and processing, removes FBI CJIS third-party cloud audit requirements for stored data, and ensures data remains available even if vendor relationships change. Estimated 7-year total cost of ownership is 38% lower than equivalent managed cloud deployments.

02 Facility Specification — ArtCraft Building
Location & Space Allocation

The data center occupies a dedicated 2,800 square foot raised-floor computer room on the second floor of the ArtCraft Building (1265 Huron Road E, Cleveland, OH 44115), within the secured CPD headquarters zone. The room is adjacent to the Real Time Crime Center (RTCC) operations floor, enabling direct fiber interconnect with sub-millisecond latency to CommandCentral Aware workstations.

Cooling Architecture

The GPU racks (GPU-01, GPU-02) use rear-door heat exchangers (RDHx) — Airedale CoolAisle XR units — rated at 35kW per rack. Chilled water supply is sourced from the ArtCraft building HVAC chiller plant, supplemented by a dedicated Liebert DSE precision cooling unit (80kW) for the computer room. All non-GPU racks are cooled by in-row Liebert iCOM precision air handlers maintaining 65–75°F inlet air per ASHRAE A2.
